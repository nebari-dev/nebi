<section class="nebi-feature-grid" aria-label="Nebi capabilities">
  <article>
    <h2>Local-First Workspaces</h2>
    <p>Track and manage Pixi workspaces locally. Initialize projects, add dependencies, and work offline with full control over your environments.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h2>Sync and Share</h2>
    <p>Push workspace specs to a Nebi server and pull them on another machine. Share reproducible environments with your team using simple version tags.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h2>Diff and Publish</h2>
    <p>Compare workspace specs across versions or directories. Publish environments to OCI registries for distribution and reproducibility.</p>
  </article>
</section>
